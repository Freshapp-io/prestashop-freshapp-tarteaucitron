Installation guide · version 1.5.1

Installing the module

  1. In the back office, open Modules > Module Manager.
  2. Click Upload a module, at the top right of the screen.
  3. Drag the .zip file you downloaded after your purchase into the drop area, without unzipping it.
  4. Wait for the installation to finish, then click Configure.
You can reach the screen again at any time from Shop Parameters > Tarteaucitron, or from the Configure button in the module manager.

After installation

The consent banner shows from installation. Choose CDN or local cache mode, then enable the third-party services the shop uses.

Full documentation

The merchant guide and the technical documentation are published online:

https://shop.freshapp.io/en/content/11-tarteaucitron

Updating: just drop the new archive through the same path. PrestaShop detects the version and runs the upgrade; your settings are kept.
